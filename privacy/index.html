Mass Gen Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 4, 2026

Mass Gen is a desktop application used to create and schedule social video posts. This page exists to support OAuth connections for services such as Instagram.

Information We Process

When you connect Instagram, Mass Gen receives OAuth authorization data from Instagram, including an access token and account identifier. This information is used only to schedule and publish content that you request from the desktop app.

Storage

OAuth tokens and connected account metadata are stored locally on your computer by the Mass Gen desktop app. This GitHub Pages callback site does not store OAuth tokens, account data, uploaded videos, or analytics data.

Use of Information

Connected account information is used to authenticate API requests, schedule posts, publish videos, and refresh access tokens where supported by the platform.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared with platform APIs only as necessary to perform actions you request, such as publishing to Instagram.

Revocation and Deletion

You can remove connected accounts in the Mass Gen app. You can also revoke access from your Instagram or Meta account settings. Removing an account deletes locally stored OAuth data for that account.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app owner through the GitHub account that hosts this page.
